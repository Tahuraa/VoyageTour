## One important change to  schema

Looking at these packages, I would make **one small improvement** to  `TourPackage` schema.

Right now we have:

```js
included_services: [String]
```

That's fine for things like:

```text
Airport transfers
Breakfast
Hotel accommodation
```

But don't put activities there. Activities should **only exist inside the itinerary**, because that's what allows  customization system to work.

So:

```text
included_services
    ↓
Airport transfer
Breakfast
Hotel accommodation
Travel insurance

itinerary
    ↓
Day 1
  Activity A
  Activity B

Day 2
  Activity C
  Activity D
```

Then when the user removes **Activity C**, your app can simply remove it from their `CustomizedTour`.

### Also, don't create a separate package for every combination.

For example, i  don't need:

> Thailand Explorer + No Wat Arun

> Thailand Explorer + Sunset Cruise

> Thailand Explorer + Deluxe Hotel

Keep **one original `TourPackage`** and let `CustomizedTour` store the user's changes.

That's what will make  customization feature actually useful.
